About
AI / ML Global Trade Entrepreneurship Data Science

Hi, I'm Anthony.

I'm the founder and CEO of Cavela — an AI platform that automates global product sourcing. I grew up across Southeast Asia's major trade hubs — Malaysia, Hong Kong, Thailand, Singapore, China — where my family has worked in the materials trade for three generations. I then spent roughly a decade living and working in Latin America. That combination of lived experience on both sides of global commerce is the lens through which I build.

My graduate research at McGill applied machine learning to global trade and maritime networks. That work was published on the front cover of Nature Sustainability — forecasting how the expansion of global shipping would reshape biological invasion risk worldwide through 2050. It was an early signal that the biggest patterns in global commerce were predictable, if you had the right models.

After McGill, I moved to San Francisco and spent several years building AI and product teams at technology startups — including Forcemetrics and Tierra. In 2023, I started Cavela after helping a family member source custom products for a project in Thailand. I built a small tool, then realized the problem was orders of magnitude larger than I'd imagined.

Cavela has since raised $8.6M across two seed rounds — backed by XYZ Venture Capital, Susa Ventures, Propel Ventures, and Go Global Ventures — and has been covered by TechCrunch, among others. We're building the infrastructure that lets any brand manufacture anywhere in the world.
