About
AI / ML Global Trade Entrepreneurship Data Science

Hi, I'm Anthony.

I'm the founder and CEO of Cavela — the world's first fully automated AI agent for global product sourcing. My family has been in the materials trade for three generations, and I've spent over 20 years living in manufacturing hubs across Asia and Latin America.

Before Cavela, I built AI and product teams at technology startups in San Francisco. I hold an MSc from McGill University, where I published research applying machine learning models to global trade prediction. I've also worked as a data scientist at ACLED, applying data science to problems in ecological invasion, sustainable development, land restoration, and maritime transport.

The idea for Cavela came when a family member needed to source custom products for a hotel project in Thailand. I built a small programme to help — and realized I had found something much bigger.
