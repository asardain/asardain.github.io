About
AI / ML Global Trade Entrepreneurship Data Science

Hi, I'm Anthony.

Founder and CEO of Cavela — AI that automates global product sourcing. I grew up across Southeast Asia's trade hubs in a family with three generations in the materials trade, then spent a decade in Latin America. My MSc research at McGill applied machine learning to global shipping networks and was published on the cover of Nature Sustainability.

I founded Cavela in 2023 after experiencing firsthand how broken global sourcing is for any brand without a dedicated procurement team. We've raised $8.6M backed by XYZ Venture Capital and Susa Ventures, and we're building the infrastructure that lets any brand manufacture anywhere in the world.
