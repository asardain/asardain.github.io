About
AI / ML Global Trade Entrepreneurship Data Science

Hi, I'm Anthony.

I'm the founder and CEO of Cavela — a global product sourcing platform powered by AI agents. My family has been in the materials trade for three generations. I grew up across major Asian trade centers — Malaysia, Hong Kong, Thailand, Singapore, and mainland China — which gave me a deep, firsthand understanding of how manufacturers and supply chains actually work.

Before founding Cavela in 2023, I was the data science lead at Tierra, a startup applying data analysis to problems in Latin America. I also spent time building AI and product teams at other technology startups in San Francisco. I hold an MSc from McGill University, where I developed machine learning models to predict global trade patterns and commodity flows. Earlier, I worked as a data scientist at ACLED, applying data science to ecological invasion, sustainable development, land restoration, and maritime transport.

The idea for Cavela came when a family member needed to source custom products for a project in Thailand. I built a small tool to help — and realized the problem was much bigger than I'd imagined.
